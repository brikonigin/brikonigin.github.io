Recruiting Operations and DEI Strategy

Brianna treats diversity, equity, and inclusion as an operational discipline rather than a branding exercise. In her view, equitable hiring comes from the system itself: how roles are defined, where candidates are sourced, how interviews are structured, and how decisions are measured. Her work focuses on:

  • Structured, consistent interviews. Scorecards and rubrics that evaluate every candidate against the same criteria.
  • Sourcing infrastructure audits. Reviewing source-of-hire data to find where referral-heavy or network-only hiring narrows the candidate pool.
  • Inclusive job descriptions and employer brand. Skills-based role definitions and candidate-facing content that reach a broader market.
  • AI hiring tool oversight. Examining how screening tools and human decisions work together across the full pipeline.
  • Compliance-aware hiring. Keeping inclusion work aligned with Title VII, EEO-1 and OFCCP requirements, and current EEOC guidance.

The Kaleidoscope Strategy

The Kaleidoscope Strategy is Brianna's framework for finding bias in hiring. Like the pattern in a kaleidoscope, bias rarely lives in a single tool or step. It appears in the pattern that the tools, people, and decisions form together, so the whole pipeline has to be examined at once.
